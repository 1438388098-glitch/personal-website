## Exam prep

Bar exam preparation moves forward. The wrong-answer pipeline now runs end to end: one full run processes 18 subjects, 41 units and 1,655 unique missed questions, takes about 9 minutes per scrape, and produces 18 notebook PDFs ready for recitation. Scale and method are documented in the [exam section](/en/exam/).

## Building

v0.1 of statute-rag, clause-scope and legal-hallu-guard is live; legal-hallu-guard has moved to v0.2, ingesting real corpora and producing a wrong-citation-rate baseline. Progress beyond that is gated on resources: statute-rag v0.2 is queued behind the Chinese embedding channel; clause-scope's real-contract evaluation (target: 30+ contracts) is waiting on corpus; judgment-struct is blocked on access to judgment corpora, channel still open.
