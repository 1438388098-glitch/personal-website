The bibliographic information is compiled from the file names of my local literature library: author and publication details for the Chinese journal articles remain to be verified one by one before being filled in, and dash subtitles in the file names are transcribed as colons on this site.

## Foreign-language sources

- Borgogno & Martino (2024), *DAO Beyond Hype*: past the hype narrative, a sober look at where DAOs actually stand in law.
- Cerna (2024), *DAO Legal Personality* (doctoral thesis): whether and how a DAO can acquire legal personality.
- DAWO (2024), *DAO Research Trends*: a review of trends in DAO research.
- *German and Turkish DAO Liability* (Springer): a comparison of how Germany and Turkey attribute liability for DAOs.
- Guillaume & Riva (2025), *DAO Legal Landscape*, chapter 1: an overall picture of the DAO legal landscape.
- Korotana (2025), *DAO LLP*: an institutional attempt to house a DAO in the form of a limited liability partnership (LLP).
- Napieralska (2024), *DAO Participant Liability*: how the liability of DAO participants is determined.
- Reyes & Hurt (2025), *Contractarian Joint Venture*: reading the legal structure of a DAO through a contractarian joint-venture frame.
- Law Commission of England and Wales (2024), *DAO Scoping Paper*: the official UK scoping report on the legal questions around DAOs.
- Udianskyi (2025), *DAO Genesis*: the origin and nature of the DAO (in Ukrainian).
- Yanagida (2025), *DAO Legal Wrapper*: paths for giving a DAO a legal wrapper under Japanese law.
- 《AOI-OPEN：基于去中心化自治组织的可信智能自动光学检测生态的联邦操作与控制》(AOI-OPEN: federated operation and control of a trustworthy intelligent automated optical inspection ecosystem built on decentralized autonomous organizations): an engineering scheme that governs a distributed industrial inspection network with a DAO (in English).

## Chinese-language sources

- 《人工智能体法律主体地位的法理反思》: a jurisprudential reflection on granting artificial intelligence agents legal subject status.
- 《人工智能法律主体论：现在与未来》: the current state and the direction of the debate over the legal subjecthood of artificial intelligence.
- 《区块链与人工智能组织体法人化理路探正》: an examination and rectification of the argumentative path toward legal-person status for blockchain and AI organizations.
- 《区块链与法律的互动：挑战、规制与融合》: the challenges blockchain poses to law, and the two-way interplay of regulation and integration.
- 《区块链技术应用的法理解构与规范进路》: legal-theoretical deconstruction and regulatory paths for blockchain applications.
- 《去中心化自治组织对公司治理的赋能与创新》: the institutional space and the limits of application once DAO mechanisms enter corporate governance.
- 《去中心化自治组织法律性质的解释论与立法论》: characterizing the DAO along the twin tracks of interpretation and legislation.
- 《去中心化自治组织的法律主体性思考：立法实践、规则构建与中国应对》: the legislative developments around DAO subjecthood and China's response.
- 《去中心化自治组织的法律性质及归责路径》: the DAO's legal characterization and the paths for attributing liability.
- 《去中心化自治组织的法治化：从组织拟制到实质控制》: a rule-of-law path for DAO governance, from formal fiction to substantive control.
- 《基于区块链的去中心化自治组织投票参与驱动机制研究》: the driving mechanisms of member voting participation in blockchain-based DAOs.
- 《算法化“主体”：组织抑或契约？》: the dispute over whether the algorithm-driven new organization should be characterized as an "organization" or a "contract".
- 《算法法人的理论证立及构成要素探析》: a theoretical justification for the "algorithmic legal person" and an anatomy of its constituent elements.
- 《论人工智能的法律主体性：以人工智能生成物的著作权保护为视角》: AI legal subjecthood approached through the copyright protection of AI-generated works.
- 《论去中心化自治组织的主体资格与监管路径》: determining the DAO's subject status and designing its regulatory path.
