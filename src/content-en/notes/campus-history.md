## Research question

Within the overall narrative of the "Five Institutes and Four Departments", Zhongnan Institute of Political Science and Law is a rare case: among the earliest political-science-and-law institutes to be founded, it was nonetheless dissolved in the successive institutional reorganizations after the founding of the People's Republic, and rebuilt only after reform and opening up. This course paper asks: what does this experience of early founding, early dissolution and late rebuilding mean within the "Five Institutes and Four Departments" landscape? And what internal tensions of the political-legal education system does the rise and fall of a single institution reflect?

## Method in brief

Historical-document analysis: the systematic study of the institutional history of the "Five Institutes and Four Departments" supplies the time series and the framework of institutional analysis, supplemented by typological studies of legal talent cultivation, examinations of political-legal education against the "new liberal arts" background, and official school-history materials and archival documents; every judgment rests on verifiable historical sources, so that inference never stands in for fact.
